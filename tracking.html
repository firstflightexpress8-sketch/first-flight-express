How Tracking Works

Every Shipment, Fully Visible

From the moment your parcel is picked up in Pokhara to the moment it reaches your recipient, we log every step so you always know where it stands.

1. Pickup Confirmed

Your parcel is scanned at pickup and enters our tracking system with your unique ID.

2. In Transit

Status updates as your shipment clears our origin hub, international transfer point, and destination customs.

3. Delivered

Final delivery is confirmed with a timestamp, and where available, a recipient signature.
