Our Story

From a Local Courier Desk to an International Network

First Flight Express started in Chipledhunga, Pokhara, with a small team and a single mission: help Nepali families and businesses send parcels abroad without the confusion, delays, or hidden costs that came with existing options.

What began as a neighborhood courier counter has grown into a full international courier and cargo operation, connecting Pokhara to more than 20 countries through air freight partnerships, customs expertise, and a door-to-door delivery model built around accountability.

Today, we serve individuals sending gifts and documents alongside exporters and e-commerce sellers who depend on us for consistent, on-time cargo consolidation and logistics support.
